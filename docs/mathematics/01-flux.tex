\section{General quadratic flux}

Let
\[
U=(u,v)\in\R^2,
\qquad
F(U)=\begin{pmatrix}f(u,v)\\ g(u,v)\end{pmatrix},
\]
with
\begin{align*}
f(u,v)&=a_{20}u^2+a_{11}uv+a_{02}v^2+a_{10}u+a_{01}v,\\
g(u,v)&=b_{20}u^2+b_{11}uv+b_{02}v^2+b_{10}u+b_{01}v.
\end{align*}
Thus the model has ten coefficients. Its Jacobian is
\[
DF(u,v)=
\begin{pmatrix}
2a_{20}u+a_{11}v+a_{10} & a_{11}u+2a_{02}v+a_{01}\\
2b_{20}u+b_{11}v+b_{10} & b_{11}u+2b_{02}v+b_{01}
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The preset inherited from the earlier quadratic example is only a particular point in this ten-parameter family:
\[
a_{20}=\frac{B_1+1}{2},\quad a_{11}=0,\quad a_{02}=\frac12,
\quad a_{10}=a,\quad a_{01}=0,
\]
\[
b_{20}=0,\quad b_{11}=1,\quad b_{02}=-\frac{B_2}{2},
\quad b_{10}=c,\quad b_{01}=a.
\]
