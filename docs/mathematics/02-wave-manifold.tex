\section{Wave manifold}

The wave manifold is defined from the Rankine--Hugoniot relation
\[
F(U_+)-F(U_-)=s(U_+-U_-).
\]
For the finite directional chart used by the application, write
\[
r(z)=\binom{z}{1},
\qquad
U_+-U_-=Y\,r(z).
\]
The midpoint is
\[
\bar U=\frac{U_-+U_+}{2},
\]
so that
\[
U_-=\bar U-\frac{Y}{2}r(z),
\qquad
U_+=\bar U+\frac{Y}{2}r(z).
\]

Because the flux is quadratic, the Rankine--Hugoniot relation reduces to the characteristic condition at the midpoint,
\[
DF(\bar U)r=s r.
\]
Eliminating $s$ gives one affine equation for $\bar U=(u,v)$,
\[
P(z)u+Q(z)v+R(z)=0,
\]
where
\begin{align*}
P(z)&=2b_{20}z^2+(b_{11}-2a_{20})z-a_{11},\\
Q(z)&=b_{11}z^2+(2b_{02}-a_{11})z-2a_{02},\\
R(z)&=b_{10}z^2+(b_{01}-a_{10})z-a_{01}.
\end{align*}

When $D=P^2+Q^2>0$, the application chooses the canonical section given by the orthogonal foot of this affine line,
\[
U_0(z)=-\frac{R(z)}{D(z)}\binom{P(z)}{Q(z)},
\]
and parametrizes the fiber by
\[
\boxed{\bar U(\tau,z)=U_0(z)+\tau\binom{-Q(z)}{P(z)}}.
\]
Hence $\tau$ is the coefficient of the non-normalized tangent vector $(-Q,P)$. In particular, $\tau$ is not arc length and is not one of the normalized coordinates used in earlier applications.

Finally, the speed stored by the implementation is the eigenvalue associated with $r$,
\[
s=\frac{r^{T}DF(\bar U)r}{r^{T}r}.
\]
