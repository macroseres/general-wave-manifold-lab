Global Hugoniot continuation (dev.24.7)

For a fixed state \(U_*\), the two projection fibres are \[ \mathcal H^{\mathrm{forw}}(U_*)=\pi_-^{-1}(U_*),\qquad \mathcal H^{\mathrm{back}}(U_*)=\pi_+^{-1}(U_*). \] The finite chart \((\tau,Y,z)\) is not used as a global chart near projective infinity. Continuation there uses \[ Z=1/z,\qquad \eta=\tau/Z^2,\qquad \Upsilon=Y/Z. \] A regular reciprocal point is mapped back for display only when \(Z\ne0\) by \(\tau=\eta Z^2\), \(Y=\Upsilon Z\), \(z=1/Z\).

The reciprocal curve is continuous through \(Z=0\) whenever its mathematical fibre point is regular there. The renderer deliberately keeps the \(Z<0\) and \(Z>0\) display pieces separate: the visual coordinate \(\hat z=(2/\pi)\arctan z\) has two boundary representatives, so joining \(\hat z=-1\) to \(\hat z=+1\) with a Euclidean chord would fabricate geometry.

Characteristic intersections \(P_s,P_f\in\mathcal C\) remain exact invariants computed independently of the display sampling and are not rendered as permanent markers.
